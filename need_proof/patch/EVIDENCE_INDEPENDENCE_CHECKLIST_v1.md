# Evidence Independence Checklist v1

Use this checklist before allowing high-impact authority.

## Evidence provenance

- Who generated the evidence?
- Who selected it?
- Who interpreted it?
- Who paid for it?
- Can the claimant alter, suppress, or time the evidence?
- Is there independent replication?
- Is there a negative-result channel?
- Is there an affected-party report channel?
- Are audit logs tamper-evident?
- Are falsification conditions specified?

## Automatic downgrades

Downgrade to weak verification if:

- the claimant generated and selected the evidence;
- only the claimant can interpret the evidence;
- the evidence is not reproducible;
- the failure condition is undefined;
- the claimant controls monitoring or stop conditions;
- evidence of harm can be suppressed;
- the evidence is a future proxy claim that cannot be checked.

## Core rule

If evidence independence is weak, authority must be capped even when the claim is morally urgent.
