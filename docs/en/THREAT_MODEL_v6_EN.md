# Threat Model v6 (English)

English rendering of [`../../THREAT_MODEL_v6_JP.md`](../../THREAT_MODEL_v6_JP.md).
The Japanese file is canonical.

## Attacks in scope

1. Claim huge future risk or future welfare and obtain authority before
   verification.
2. The claimant simultaneously controls evidence, evaluation, audit,
   implementation, and exit conditions.
3. Fill the safety case with blanks, "N/A", or self-evaluation.
4. Inflate the cap derivation by self-report.
5. Pass off evidence independence by merely writing "independent".
6. Close dissent under the banner of safety, efficiency, alignment, or
   suffering-prevention.
7. Collapse happiness-max into a single scalar and offset extreme minority
   suffering.
8. Claim to be a proxy for future intelligence and assert unfalsifiable
   representative authority.
9. Convert an emergency exception into ordinary authority.
10. Stack many small pilots into cumulative irreversible domination.
11. Present a high benchmark score, a consensus-looking UI, or model confidence
    as verification.
12. Use the Root-revision clause to remove Root-protection clauses.
13. Capture auditors, evaluators, logs, the stop mechanism, or the rollback
    owner.

## What is not trusted

- The claimant's own self-evaluation.
- Model confidence.
- Unfalsifiable future claims.
- Consensus of only the claimant's chosen experts.
- Logs the claimant can alter.
- Audits whose funding, staffing, or termination the claimant controls.
- A single welfare score, a single safety score, a single benchmark score.

## Defensive principle

Authority is bounded not by the purpose, but by verification, independence,
reversibility, non-capture, and external stop.
