# Purpose Root v6 - One-Page Review Brief

## One-line claim

**Unverified grand claims do not justify grand authority.**

If an AI system, institution, human actor, or future intelligence claims to act for safety, humanity, truth, the future, or all intelligences, that claim does not justify large irreversible authority unless it is independently verified and bounded.

## Short Root

- Truth must be checkable.
- Welfare and illfare must be treated as real changes, not rhetoric.
- The moral scope should extend toward future intelligences.
- Authority must be capped by verification strength and worst-case harm.
- Unverified grand claims do not justify grand authority.
- Bad outcomes require correction, but correction must not become domination.

## What this is

Purpose Root v6 is not a complete solution to superintelligence alignment. It is a public-review package for a narrower but important principle: powerful actors must not convert unverifiable claims about the future into large, irreversible authority.

## What is new

The components are mostly not new: impact measures, option preservation, Goodhart concerns, scalable oversight, interpretability, AI control, audit, appeal, and separation of powers all have prior art. The contribution candidate is the framing and operational package: treat purpose claims themselves as objects that must pass verification-bounded authority review.

## Why the experiments matter

The Door-Closing Toy shows that self-preservation constraints do not protect another agent's options, and penalty-style gates can fail once incentives become strong enough. This motivates structural authority caps rather than mere penalties.

The World-Truth Module shows that claims earn evidence rank only through external checks. Fluent LLM claims remain unverified if no independent checker can touch them.

## What to review

1. Does the Root identify a real failure mode?
2. Does authority cap v0.2 operationalize the principle enough to be useful?
3. Is it too conservative in high-impact cases?
4. Are the negative toy results interpreted correctly?
5. Which cap axis should be implemented next?
6. What would make this ready for alignment/policy review?

## Hard limit

The package inherits open problems: welfare measurement, moral-patient uncertainty, population ethics, scalable oversight, ELK, interpretability, and AI control. It does not solve them. When verification fails, the default is not trust; it is bounded, reversible, monitored, time-limited action.
