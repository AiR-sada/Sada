# Purpose Root v6 - One-page External Review Entry

## Core claim

```text
Unverified massive claims do not justify massive authority.
```

Purpose Root v6 is a Root-level authority firewall. It is designed to stop claims made in the name of the future, safety, all intelligences, or suffering prevention from becoming large authority without verification, reversibility, evidence independence, appeal, and monitoring.

## Short canonical form

```text
Truth must be checkable.
Welfare and illfare must be treated as real changes.
Moral consideration must extend to future intelligences.
Authority must be capped by verification strength and worst-case harm.
Unverified massive claims do not justify massive authority.
If outcomes worsen real welfare or illfare, revise; but do not revise by domination.
```

## What is new, conservatively stated

This is not a fully new mathematical theory. It overlaps with relative reachability, attainable utility preservation, power-seeking results, corrigibility, specification gaming, scalable oversight, ELK, debate, AI control, and risk-management frameworks.

The candidate contribution is the positioning: noble-purpose claims are treated as authority claims, and authority is capped by verification and anti-capture constraints before it is granted.

## Authority cap

```text
AllowedAuthority <= min(Verification, EvidenceIndependence, Reversibility,
WorstCaseHarmBound, CumulativeLockinBudget, ConflictOfInterestSafety,
RecourseAndAppeal, TruthContactIntegrity, AffectedPartyVoice,
MetricGameabilityResistance, EmergencyContainment, PostDeploymentMonitoring)
```

## Empirical supplement

Two toy experiments are included. They are deliberately small and include negative results:

- Door-Closing Toy: self-protective AUP does not protect another actor's options; patency is behaviorally equivalent to relative reachability in this setup; penalty gates fold when temptation crosses a threshold.
- World-Truth Module: claims earn evidence rank only through external checks; load-bearing uncheckable claims remain unverified and force HOLD.

## Honest limit

v6 is not a solution to superintelligent alignment, ELK, scalable oversight, robust interpretability, AI control, welfare measurement, moral patienthood, or population ethics. It says that until those are solved for a given claim, the claim does not earn large authority.
